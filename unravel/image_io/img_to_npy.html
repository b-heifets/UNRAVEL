unravel.image_io.img_to_npy module#

Use io_img_to_npy (i2np) from UNRAVEL to convert a 3D image to an ndarray and save it as a .npy file.

Usage:#

io_img_to_npy -i path/to/image.czi -o path/to/image.npy [-ao zyx] [-v]
